“God’s Mixed Feelings About Jewelry”: A Critical Review

By Pastor Wentland Paul · Published July 28, 2026 · Reviewed August 2, 2026

A critical sermon review of Dr. Alexander Biscette’s sermon on jewelry. It examines the sermon’s governing hermeneutic, its wilderness argument, its reading of currency and bridewealth texts, and its rhetorical disclaimers of judgment — and finds that a single controlling interpretive key is applied recursively across dissimilar texts rather than a convergent witness.
